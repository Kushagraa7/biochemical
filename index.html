How to learn a procedure

1. Say the sample and reagent amounts aloud.

2. Remember time and temperature.

3. Predict the observation before revealing it.

4. Explain why that colour/change happens.

Safety reminder

This is a study aid, not an instruction to perform experiments alone. Concentrated acids, NaOH, organic solvents, hot water baths and chemical reagents require instructor supervision, PPE and the lab's safety procedure.
